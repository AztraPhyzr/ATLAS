    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 17, 2026

    History Atlas is a standalone browser demonstration. This Privacy Policy explains what this version stores and how it is handled.

    Information stored

    When you create an account, the email address you provide and a salted password hash are saved in this browser's local storage. Your plain-text password is not stored. The signed-in email is kept in session storage. Information, notes, sources, timeline entries, and images that you contribute are also saved in local storage.

    Storage, sharing, and access

    This version does not upload accounts or contributions to an Atlas server, synchronize them between devices, or publish or share them with other visitors. Anyone who can access your browser profile or device may be able to access its locally stored data. Avoid entering sensitive personal information and use care on shared devices.

    Retention and deletion

    Contributions can be removed using the site's delete controls. Account records remain in the browser until its site data is cleared. Data is not backed up on a server and cannot be recovered by Atlas if it is cleared or lost.

    Security and external requests

    Password hashes do not make this local demo equivalent to secure server authentication. Do not reuse passwords from other services. When online, the page may request fonts from Google Fonts; those font requests are separate from the account and contribution data stored locally.

    Changes and contact

    This policy describes the current standalone demo. If online accounts, synchronization, or sharing are introduced, the policy should be updated before those features are used. This demo has no online account support or dedicated privacy-contact service.
